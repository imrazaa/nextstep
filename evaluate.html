Frequently Asked Questions

How accurate is this evaluation?

It's a quick estimate based on typical published requirements for each country, meant to give you a starting point. Actual requirements vary by university, program, and intake, so we always confirm the exact details with you during a consultation.

Is my information saved anywhere?

Yes, we save your evaluation answers anonymously for statistics, this never includes your name, email, or any other identifying details, only the same fields you entered here (country, degree, CGPA, test score, and experience). We only connect an evaluation to you personally if you choose to register your interest afterward.

Can I evaluate my profile for more than one country?

Yes, just change the target country and submit again, your results will update instantly.

What if I haven't taken an English test yet?

Leave the score field blank and select "Not taken yet", we'll flag this in your results and can help you plan your IELTS preparation as part of any package.
